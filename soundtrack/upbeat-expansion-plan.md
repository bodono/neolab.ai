# Upbeat expansion plan — four more delivery-day-class tracks

**Status:** shipped — all four tracks are composed, rendered, and wired; rotation shipped as shuffled pools rather than a fixed order. Follow-up to track 11, *The GPUs Arrive on a
Tuesday*, whose brief was: higher tempo, more optimistic, more fun, still
impossible to be startled by.

## Shared rules (inherited from track 11's post-beep discipline)

- Melody tops out at B5; bells stay mid-register; no tick blips anywhere.
- Percussion at or below the *Gradients* light-kit level; comfort verified
  spectrally against *Gradients* (2–5 kHz share) before a track ships.
- Every track gets exactly one **signature fun mechanism** — a musical joke it
  owns, the way track 11 owns the unboxing sweep — plus the §4.1 arrangement
  standard (intro, development, breathing bar(s), return, loop landing).
- Each new track states the Lab Motif once, coloured by its own mode.
- Keys and tempos are chosen to avoid every existing track's combination.

## The four tracks

### 12 · All Tests Pass on the First Try — 128 BPM, C major, 80 bars (2:30)

The rarest joy in software. C major with no accidentals at all: nothing needs
fixing. **Signature mechanism: a two-beat canon** — the rounded pluck imitates
the lead exactly two beats behind and lands cleanly every single time, like a
CI pipeline of stages going green one after another. The intro plants one
worried out-of-key tone (the test everyone expected to fail), resolves it
sweetly, and never plays it again. Voices enter one per section like passing
stages. Rotation: general and research playlists.

### 13 · New Hire Orientation — 124 BPM, F Lydian, 80 bars (2:35)

First-day energy: badge photo, wrong door, learning everyone's names. The
Lydian raised fourth is the wide-eyed interval. **Signature mechanism: a
melody that learns.** Phrase A first appears hesitant — gaps where notes
should be, one landing in the wrong octave — and each restatement fills a gap
until the return section finally plays it whole and confident. A mentor/newbie
duet runs underneath: warm-lead states, pluck echoes simplified, and by the
bridge the pluck keeps up in parallel harmony. Rotation: general and research;
prefer selection after the `researcher-joins` cue (presentation-only, same
pattern as *Reviewer Two* after paper cues).

### 14 · The Demo Worked Twice — 134 BPM, G Mixolydian, 80 bars (2:23)

It worked in rehearsal AND on stage. The fastest non-crisis track, one notch
above Tuesday, on Tuesday's crooked-grin mode transposed to G. **Signature
mechanism: the demo phrase performed twice** — first intimate (solo lead over
thin pads, the rehearsal), then, after a single held-breath bar of pads alone,
the full-band restatement (the stage run), landing cleanly both times. Short
soft pad stabs on offbeats stand in for applause without any transient. 
Rotation: general and commercial.

### 15 · Compute Budget Approved — 120 BPM, E major, 72 bars (2:24)

Finance said yes. A strutting, warm celebration at the calm end of upbeat.
**Signature mechanism: the adding-machine ostinato** — a strict quarter-note
pluck like a ledger being totted up, which loosens into syncopation the moment
the harmony "signs off" (a rising inversion of the Safety Answer motif as the
approval stamp). A walking triangle bass — stepwise, jaunty, distinct from
Tuesday's octave bounce — carries the strut. Rotation: commercial and general.

## Rotation after all four (gameplay-integration §7 update to ship with them)

| Focus | Pool (signature first; playback shuffles with no immediate repeats) |
|---|---|
| General | gradients → tests-pass → hello-world → peer-reviewer → overnight-run → demo-worked-twice → cashflow → gpus-arrive-tuesday |
| Commercial | cashflow → gpus-arrive-tuesday → budget-approved → demo-worked-twice → gradients |
| Research | peer-reviewer → tests-pass → gradients → new-hire-orientation → overnight-run → safety-case |
| Safety | safety-case → peer-reviewer → overnight-run → hello-world → gradients |

Safety deliberately gains none of the new tracks: its rotation stays
reflective, and the upbeat family should feel like a reward for ordinary
healthy operation rather than wallpaper.

## Delivery checklist per track (same as tracks 10–11)

renderer composer + spec → render m4a/opus → spectral comfort check →
manifest → audition.html → README / design bible / prototypes README /
gameplay-integration → apps/web types, catalogue, playlists → test counts →
in-browser audition verification.
