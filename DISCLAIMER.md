## Removal requests

Several characters in Neolab.ai are affectionate parodies inspired by the public professional
lives of real researchers and laboratory leaders. The portrayals are intended to be admiring, and
the game is distributed free of charge.

If you are, or represent, a person whose name, likeness, or career inspired a character, and you
would prefer not to be included, email **<bodonoghue85@gmail.com>** with the character's name.
The character will be removed or renamed, on a best-effort basis within 30 days, without argument
and without any requirement to explain or justify the request.

The same applies to any organisation that considers itself depicted, and to anything in the game
you believe is inaccurate about you: corrections are as welcome as removals.
