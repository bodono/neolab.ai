# Prototype listening copies

These files are deterministic full-length composition and synthesis renders, not final masters.

**Current mix:** Complete Alpha. The shared foreground motifs use a rounded harmonic lead with a
gentler attack and lower gain. Kicks no longer contain a noise click, while snares and hats use
filtered noise with slower attacks and substantially less high-frequency energy. The global C.1
comfort pass removes raw pulse, hard square, and quantised chip voices from every track. Bright
hats and noisy snare cracks are also excluded; the remaining percussion is intentionally quiet,
low-passed, and rounded.

`The Gradients Are Flowing` replaces its continuous pulse arpeggio with a rounded harmonic
pluck, lowers its drum levels, and reduces the busiest hat pattern from eighth notes to off-beat
quarter notes. The same no-abrasion rule now applies across the complete score. In particular,
`Red Team Found Something` replaces its rapid pulse bed and bright high-register figure, thins its
percussion pattern, and uses tension from harmony rather than sonic aggression.

All nine catalogue entries are full arrangements. Tracks 01–08 are designed to loop; Track 09 is a
non-looping ending.

Recommended listening order:

1. `01-hello-world-model.m4a` — title and lab selection
2. `02-gradients-flowing.m4a` — normal laboratory play
3. `03-safety-case-draft-47.m4a` — reflective safety work
4. `04-red-team-found-something.m4a` — crisis
5. `05-last-evaluation.m4a` — endgame
6. `06-broadly-shared-future.m4a` — victory
7. `07-cashflow-positive.m4a` — commercial momentum
8. `08-peer-reviewer-two.m4a` — publication and research archive
9. `09-nothing-left-to-read.m4a` — extinction ending

Tracks 01–08 are complete loops. AAC encoder padding may make consumer-player looping imperfect;
the uncompressed renderer output and exact musical bar count remain the source for future
sample-accurate Web Audio looping.

Listen for whether sections develop naturally, whether any passage outstays its welcome, and
whether each loop return feels musical. Track 09 should instead feel complete when it ends.
